About Me

Belayneh Endalamaw Dejene is an accomplished data scientist, artificial intelligence researcher, and university lecturer with an extensive background in information science, predictive modeling, machine learning, and healthcare analytics. Belayneh began his academic journey by obtaining a Bachelor's degree in Information Science from the University of Gondar, Ethiopia, in 2018. He then completed a Master of Science in Data Science and Analytics at the same university in 2021 with distinction.

Currently, Belayneh is pursuing his PhD in Data Science at Bahir Dar University's Institute of Technology (since 2022). He is also serving as a Data Scientist and Analyst at the Armauer Hansen Research Institute (AHRI) under the Data Science Without Borders (DSWB) project, supported by the African Population and Health Research Center (APHRC).

Belayneh serves as Co-Investigator and Lead AI / Machine Learning Engineer on the prestigious March of Dimes Discovery Research Grant titled "AI-Enhanced Dynamic Risk Stratification and Risk-Responsive Antenatal Care to Prevent Spontaneous Preterm Birth in Ethiopia: A Cluster Randomized Controlled Trial." In this grant, he leads data engineering, predictive feature development, and model integration into the clinical decision-support system.

During his tenure as a Lecturer and Researcher at the University of Gondar College of Informatics, Belayneh has taught undergraduate courses in programming, data structures, algorithms, databases, and applied machine learning, while serving on academic committees, curriculum modernization, and technology transfer initiatives. He previously served as a Data Analyst at the Ethiopian Commodity Exchange (ECX), conducting quantitative price volatility analyses.

Belayneh has published 9 peer-reviewed research papers in international journals including HIV Medicine, BMC Public Health, BMC Infectious Diseases, BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth, Italian Journal of Pediatrics, BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making, and IEEE NIGERCON.

Research areas: Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Explainable & Interpretable AI (XAI), Clinical Decision Support Systems, Healthcare Analytics, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, and Digital Twin Simulation for people living with HIV.
